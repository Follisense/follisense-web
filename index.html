Who it is for

Textured hair,
taken seriously

FolliSense is built around protective styles, takedown days, retwists and barber visits, because tension, buildup and shrinkage are the things that actually shape scalp health for coily and curly hair.

That means the questions, the timing and the guidance are written for type 3 and type 4 patterns. If your hair is straight or wavy, type 1 or type 2, you are welcome here and the photo record and check-ins work the same way. Just know that some routine advice is shaped around wash days and styles that may not be yours.

For men

A cut every week is
a cycle too

Most hair apps have nothing for men beyond a hairline photo. FolliSense tracks the parts that actually repeat: the barber chair, the shave, the line-up, and what your scalp does in between.

Cuts and barber visits

Log a fade, a line-up or a full cut and check in around it, so you are comparing the same scalp at the same point in the cycle rather than whenever you remember.

Razor bumps and irritation

Bumps, ingrown hairs and soreness after a shave get logged as their own thing, so you can see whether they follow a particular barber, blade or product.

Hairline and crown

Guided photos from the same angles each time, so a receding line or a thinning crown is something you can see side by side instead of guessing at in the mirror.

Fades & tapers Line-ups Waves Locs & twists Bald & shaved Growing it out

Any scale describing hairline or crown change is a way of recording what you can see, not a diagnosis or a prediction. Hair loss has many causes. If you are worried about it, see a doctor or dermatologist.

Reminders

The style has been in
longer than you think

Tell FolliSense when a style went in and how long you plan to keep it. You get a nudge as takedown week approaches, and again if it runs past what you planned, because four weeks has a way of quietly becoming seven.

The same works for retwists, wash days and barber visits. Reminders are built from what you told us, and they are a convenience rather than a rule. If a style feels tight, itchy or sore, do not wait for a notification.

Foli

Automated assistant · replies instantly

My braids are 3 weeks in and my scalp is flaking. Wash or wait?

Three weeks is a normal point for buildup. A diluted clarifying wash applied at the scalp, then a light moisturiser, usually settles it without disturbing the braids.

Will that make them frizzy?

Some, yes. Applying with a nozzle bottle and drying fully afterwards keeps it to a minimum.

Foli is automated and can be wrong. It is not medical advice and not a substitute for seeing a professional.
